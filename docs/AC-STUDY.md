# What Ebb takes from Animal Crossing: New Horizons

Studied 22 October 2026 from public write-ups (Audiokinetic's blog on the game's spatial audio, Nintendo Life on
soundscapes, WhatCulture on small details, Real Homes on room design, a HAW Hamburg thesis on cosy games). These are
the rules every Ebb workstream follows. The YouTube tips video Jamie sent (Crossing Channel, "Tips & Tricks For
2026") could not be transcribed tonight: YouTube rate-limited the subtitle download. Its description says it covers
the 3.0 update's additions; worth a second look when the limit lifts.

## The world is solid
- Nothing is walked through or over. Every painted object is a blocked area, with a margin above it so she is never
  drawn in front of something she is standing behind.
- Rugs and mats are the only things she walks on.
- A tap on a thing walks her to its stand point and only then does the thing respond.

## One picture per place
- A room is one whole render, not sprites pasted on a shell. Pieces only come apart when she chooses to redecorate,
  and that mode shows the empty shell with her own arrangement.
- The camera is the room camera: high, looking down at about 55 degrees, side walls visible, the floor open in the
  middle front. Every place is a tall 4:7 plate so the phone shows the whole width.

## The character
- Oversized round head about the size of the body, stubby limbs, big oval eyes, a small smile. One size in every
  outfit. Walk cycles in three directions, a sit, and work poses.
- Reactions: a bubble above her head when she does something (a note while watering, a sparkle when she picks, a
  heart when she sits). Small, short, never blocking.

## Sound is positional and surface-aware
- The waterfall swells as she nears the pond. Birds are loudest among the trees. Indoors has a quiet room tone.
- Footsteps change with the ground: boards, tile, soil, grass.
- Soft interface sounds: a pop when a sheet opens, a tick on a tap, a sparkle on a reward, a swoosh on a door.
- Everything synthesised, gentle, and off until she turns it on.

## Time passes
- The phone clock sets dawn, day, golden hour, dusk and night outdoors, and a lamp glow indoors.
- Some days it rains. Fireflies come out at night by the water.

## Small life everywhere
- Twinkling fairy lights, drifting motes, falling petals, steam off a bath, mist at the falls.
- Nothing wilts, dies or punishes. Growth happens overnight. Progress never depends on what she logs.
