# How farming, fishing and food fit together (EveRhy, 24 Oct 2026)

One currency, **hearts**, earned by caring for yourself: logging how you are (10), finishing a practice (15) and Today's
invitations (10 each), up to 60 a day. Everything else in the world is spent or made from them.

1. **Seeds**: buy with hearts at the Flower Dome stall (turnip 20, carrot 30, sunflower 40).
2. **Garden**: hoe a bed, plant, water once a day. It grows one stage for each watered day and is ready in 2 to 4 days.
   Nothing wilts or dies. Harvest goes to the basket.
3. **Fishing**: at the end of Bedok Jetty. Wait for the nibble, reel in. Then choose: keep it for the kitchen, or let it go.
4. **Provisions**: the fruit stall at the hawker centre sells eggs, rice, ginger and chilli (5 hearts each).
5. **Cook at home**: the hob, oven and Thermomix make dishes from the basket (soups, stir-fry, egg fried rice, steamed
   fish with ginger, sambal turnip, bakes, smoothies). A cooked dish waits in the bag under **Ready to eat**.
6. **Eat slowly**: sit at the kitchen table, A mindful moment, Eat slowly. A minute with the food; after 20 seconds the
   dish counts as eaten and the practice is saved, which earns hearts again.
7. **Wishes**: the water at Merlion Park and Little Guilin takes a quiet wish, never an item. Five days of wishes and the
   water gives a moon pearl.

The bag shows all of it: Seeds, From the garden, Caught and found, Pantry, Ready to eat, Gifts, Tools.
